# SmartExpenseAI
An intelligent personal finance assistant that helps users track expenses, analyze spending habits, plan budgets, and achieve financial goals—all in one place.

Overview

Most expense trackers stop at recording transactions. SmartExpense AI is designed to go one step further.Instead of simply showing where your money went, it helps you understand:

-Where you're overspending
-Whether you're following healthy budgeting habits
-How long it will take to achieve your financial goals
-How your spending aligns with the popular 50/30/20 budgeting rule

The long-term vision is to evolve SmartExpense AI into an AI-powered personal financial coach capable of providing personalized budgeting advice, savings recommendations, and spending insights.
